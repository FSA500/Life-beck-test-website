Our Story

Built on Principle. Driven by Results.

Leif Beck Law was founded in 2009 with a single conviction: every property owner deserves fierce, expert representation — regardless of whether they're an individual homeowner or a large commercial developer.

Over fifteen years we have grown from a solo practice into a full-service property rights firm trusted by clients across the Pacific Northwest and beyond. Our attorneys combine deep courtroom experience with a thorough understanding of local zoning codes, title law, and eminent domain procedure.

We measure success not by billable hours but by outcomes — land secured, compensation recovered, and rights restored. When your property is on the line, we treat it as if it were our own.
